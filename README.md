# Toy Story
**Toy Story** is one of my favorite movies! 
**Toy Story** is a 1995 American animated *adventure comedy* film produced by Pixar Animation Studios for Walt Disney Pictures. It is the first installment in the **Toy Story** franchise and the first entirely computer-animated feature film, as well as the first feature film from **Pixar**.

## Main Characters
- **Woody** – a pullstring cowboy doll who is Andy's favorite toy  
- **Buzz Lightyear** – a Space Ranger action figure, Andy's second favorite toy, and Woody's rival, who later becomes his best friend  
- **Mr. Potato Head** – a cynical potato-shaped doll with put-together pieces on his body 
- **Slinky Dog** – a dachshund slinky toy and Woody's second-in-command  
- **Rex** – a nervous green Tyrannosaurus figurine
- **Hamm** – a smart-talking piggy bank
- **Bo Peep** – a porcelain shepherdess doll and Woody's love interest
- **Andy Davis** – the toys' owner
